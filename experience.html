THE EXPERIENCE · NORTH GEORGIA

A stay that feels
like yours.

Mountain mornings, meaningful connections, adventures for every interest—and the freedom to take a little time for yourself.

01 / BELONGING

A place where your family just fits.

Come as you are. Meet families who understand, find familiar faces around the table, or simply enjoy being somewhere you don't have to explain yourself.

Connection is always an invitation, never an expectation.

02 / PEACE OF MIND

Thoughtful from the start.

Feeling comfortable shouldn't be something you have to figure out once you arrive. Here's how The Clearing is envisioned to make the experience more predictable and personal.

01

Before you arrive

Share interests, routines, accessibility preferences, and what helps each guest feel at home.

02

When you check in

Get a warm welcome, a clear picture of the day, and time to settle in at your own pace.

03

Throughout your stay

Choose experiences with individualized support options and clear communication along the way.

03 / THE GOOD STUFF

Fill your days with
what you love.

There isn't one right way to vacation. Wander outside, try something new, or spend the afternoon doing absolutely nothing.

OUTDOORS

Explore the mountains

Nature walks, scenic overlooks, and accessible outdoor discovery.

CREATE

Make something together

Art, hands-on workshops, and creative afternoons.

GATHER

Stay a little longer

Shared meals, fireside evenings, and unhurried conversations.

04 / ROOM TO EXHALE

Time for yourself.
Joy for them.

For parents and caregivers, a real vacation can mean knowing your family member is enjoying an experience of their own—so you can enjoy yours, too.

YOUR AFTERNOON

A little time that's yours

Book a spa treatment, linger over lunch, or take in the mountain view with no agenda.

THEIR AFTERNOON

An adventure of their own

Choose a creative workshop, outdoor discovery, or another interest-led activity with planned support options.

Different experiences. The same opportunity to enjoy the day.

05 / BACK TOGETHER

And then, the best part:
coming together.

Swap stories over dinner. Find a favorite spot by the fire. End the day with new memories—and maybe a few new friends.
